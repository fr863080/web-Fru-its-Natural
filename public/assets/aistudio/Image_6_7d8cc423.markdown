## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies a modern Mexican artesanal culinary experience, capturing the energy, warmth, and vibrant indulgence of authentic *aguas frescas*. It pairs roadside paletería charm with contemporary, high-converting digital commerce polish. 

The UI communicates tactile freshness, organic craft, and joyful sun-drenched hospitality. The aesthetic blends **tactile artisan warmth** with **clean modern commerce**:
- Deep natural wood and rich brown tones anchor the foundational structure, lending authenticity and ground to the canvas.
- Crisp, creamy vanilla paper backgrounds evoke natural parchment and clean culinary packaging.
- Punchy, fruit-saturated primary tones (citrus orange, lime/leaf greens, strawberry red) deliver vibrant visual pops across action buttons, nutritional badges, and micro-interactions.
- Organic fluid wave contours, scalloped stamp badges, and sunlit ambiance elevate the digital storefront beyond typical corporate e-commerce, creating an appetizing and trustworthy direct-to-consumer experience.

## Colors

The palette balances sunlit citrus fruits, lush garden leaves, and earthy wood tones over a warm culinary background.

- **Primary (`#F39200`) & Deep Citrus Accent (`#E06800`)**: The signature sunshine yellow-orange, applied to primary call-to-actions, cart badges, checkout triggers, and highlighted order milestones.
- **Secondary (`#5B8C2A`) & Forest Shade (`#3A6916`)**: Represents fresh agave, squeezed limes, mint, and tropical leaves. Used for secondary actions, stock availability tags, freshness guarantees, and organic certification badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D92525`)**: The ripe strawberry and hibiscus (*jamaica*) punch. Deployed for promotional flash sales, limited-run seasonal drops, urgency indicators, and critical error/alert feedback.
- **Neutral Deep Tone (`#42210B`)**: Rich cacao and toasted tamarind wood. Replaces harsh pitch-black for all typography, crisp outlines, high-contrast borders, and grounded footers.
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFDF7`) & Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Soft vanilla cream providing eye comfort and organic warmth, contrasting with crisp white card containers and warm sand borders (`#EADBCE`).

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Outfit**, chosen for its geometric precision blended with friendly, open circular apertures that reflect the playful roundness of citrus slices and handcrafted stamp seals.

- **Display & Headlines**: Bold, confident weights (700 and 800) bring punchy energy to flavor names (*Sandía Fresca*, *Horchata Artesanal*, *Limonada con Chía*) and marketing taglines.
- **Body Text**: Regular weight (400) set in deep roast cacao (`#42210B`) delivers high legibility on warm vanilla canvas backgrounds without the harshness of pure black.
- **Labels & Badges**: Medium and bold weights (600, 700) with slight letter-spacing give product badges, ingredient highlights, and checkout CTAs clear visibility and rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) centered within a maximum container width of `1280px`.

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px)**: 4 columns, `margin` of `1rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Cards and quick-order ribbons collapse to full-width horizontal sliders or vertical tap stacks.
- **Tablet Viewport (640px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Catalog items sit in 2-column arrays with quick-add actions accessible directly on cards.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, `margin-desktop` of `3rem`, `gutter-desktop` of `2rem`. Supports rich editorial side-by-side configurations: product splash photography on the left, artisan story and dynamic flavor checkout selectors on the right.
- **Vertical Flow Rhythm**: Section breaks leverage `space-xl` combined with organic wave-mask dividers to emulate fluid splashes of juice and natural liquid motion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines warm tropical ambient shadows, rich layered paper surfaces, and crisp organic borders:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Creamy vanilla surface (`#FFFDF7`) accented with faint fluid wave shapes in soft lime (`rgba(91, 140, 42, 0.08)`) or delicate orange tint (`rgba(243, 146, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Product Modules)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted ivory surfaces bordered by a subtle warm sand stroke (`1.5px solid #EADBCE`) with an ambient warm shadow (`0px 4px 14px rgba(66, 33, 11, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Sticky Cart & Overlays)**: Uplifted cards shift upward 2px with an active shadow (`0px 10px 24px rgba(66, 33, 11, 0.12)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Artisan Flavor Drawers)**: Deep layered backdrop blur (`8px`) paired with a rich depth shadow (`0px 18px 36px rgba(66, 33, 11, 0.22)`).
- **Badge & Ribbon Depth**: Authentic seal effects use scalloped outline profiles, subtle inner circular rings, and light 1px highlight borders that imitate embossed artisanal food packaging labels.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** shape scale (base `0.5rem`, large `1rem`, extra-large `1.5rem`). This provides approachable curves without sacrificing transactional clarity.

- **Standard Elements (Input fields, standard cards, dropdowns)**: Base radius `0.5rem` (8px), offering clean geometric order for tabular and e-commerce data.
- **Focus Containers (Product hero tiles, promotional boxes)**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to soften imagery and mirror fruit silhouettes.
- **Action Triggers & Dietary Chips**: Full pill profiles (`9999px`) for flavor tags (*Sin Azúcar*, *100% Natural*, *Hecho a Mano*) and checkout CTA buttons to invite immediate touch interaction.
- **Brand Stamp Shapes**: Circular seal badges with perimeter zig-zag/scallop edges evoking Mexican artisan craft seals and fruit stand quality stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Agregar al Carrito", "Comprar Ahora")**: Saturated Citrus Orange (`#F39200`), dark cacao text (`#42210B`) or crisp white (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape (`9999px`), bold weight (`700`), with an ambient orange-tinted shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(243, 146, 0, 0.35)`). Active state applies a punchy press effect (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action ("Ver Ingredientes", "Explorar Sabores")**: Deep Leaf Green (`#5B8C2A`) background or 2px outline with green text on hover; full pill shape.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Transparent surface with deep cacao underline and subtle amber highlight on hover.

### Chips & Flavor Badges
- **Taste & Dietary Tags**: Compact pill badges with soft pastel fills (e.g., lime wash `#EDF5E5` with `#3A6916` text; vanilla amber `#FEF3E2` with `#E06800` text).
- **Authenticity Stamp Badge**: Circular or notched ribbon graphic holding a central icon (lemon slice, strawberry, water drop) bounded by concentric borders, heart emblems, and arched text.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs (Delivery address, email capture)**: Crisp white container with 1.5px warm sand border (`#EADBCE`), `0.5rem` radius, and dark cacao text. Focused state transitions smoothly to a 2px Citrus Orange border with soft glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(243, 146, 0, 0.15)`).
- **Quantity Selector**: Segmented pill component featuring rounded minus/plus buttons in leaf green or warm brown flanking bold numeral typography.

### Product & Flavor Cards
- **Beverage Showcase Card**: High-contrast white card container with curved photo hero zone, floating circular flavor badge at top-right, bold product title, concise taste notes, size toggle pills (16oz / 32oz / Galón), and direct high-converting "Quick Add" floating trigger.
- **Subtle Organic Accents**: Card hover states lightly reveal decorative fruit contour wireframes or botanical corner motifs in faint leaf tones.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and rounded checkboxes in rich cacao borders (`#42210B`). When checked, fills with Lush Green (`#5B8C2A`) containing a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Checkout Conversion Strip & Mini-Cart
- Sticky bottom or right-side slide-over drawer featuring a progress tracker for free shipping thresholds, real-time freshly made batch notifications, and a prominent Citrus Orange single-click checkout button.
